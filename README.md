# Energy-efficient-home 🏡⚡

A collection of DIY electronics and firmware for an energy-efficient home. Focused on efficient energy consumption, reliable heating control, and resource optimization.

## 🎯 The Philosophy
While the ultimate goal is to build a fully integrated Smart Home, not all projects here are "smart" in the IoT sense just yet. Some of them don't have Wi-Fi or cloud connectivity. However, they all share one common trait — they are designed to make the home **highly efficient**, reliable, and optimized in its energy usage.
